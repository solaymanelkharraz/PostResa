# PostResa - Next Version Roadmap (v2.0)

1. **Reservation Engine & Group Responsibles (Délégués / Responsables de Groupe)**
   - Full Space Reservation system deployment (Reserving spaces, time slots, purpose, confirmation workflow).
   - **Responsable de Groupe (Group Delegates / Leaders)**:
     - Ability for Admins to designate specific students/members as "Responsable de Groupe".
     - Only authorized Group Responsibles (and Professors/Admins) can request/reserve spaces on behalf of their group.
   - Reverse Reservations: Admins & Professors allocating spaces directly to specific student groups.

2. **Sub-Admins & Permission Scopes**
   - Allow School Admins to create Sub-Admin accounts.
   - Assign custom permission scopes/action access per Sub-Admin (e.g., Post Moderator, Reservation Moderator, User Manager, Schedule Manager).

3. **Real-Time WebSockets & Live Alerts**
   - Live WebSockets for direct & group messaging.
   - Instant push notifications for reservation approvals/rejections and post announcements.

4. **PDF Exports & Analytics**
   - Export printable PDF schedules for groups and spaces.
   - Space usage analytics dashboard.
